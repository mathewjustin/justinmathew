Episode 01 · TLSWork in progress

Why would your browser trust my root CA?

Anyone can create a root certificate authority. This short pilot explores why creating one and getting a client to trust it are different things.

First cut · about 90 seconds · My narration, diagrams, and a replay of real OpenSSL output. Captions and a checked transcript will accompany the finished episode.

The idea to take away

A self-signature doesn’t establish trust. The client’s configured trust anchor does.

The practice

Ask a question. Make a prediction. Run the experiment. Explain what happened.
